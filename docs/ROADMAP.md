# The Long Climb — a texture & mechanics roadmap

This is the spine we walk together. Not a wishlist and not marketing — a working
backlog of **core-play texture**, ordered so we can take it one step at a time.
It's also the memory between sessions: when context resets, this file is where I
pick the thread back up.

## How we walk this

- **One item per pull request.** Small enough to read in a sitting, real enough
  to feel while climbing.
- **I bring the attempt; you rule on it.** Approve it, bend it, or bounce it.
  Then we advance the pointer to the next step. Nothing here ships without your
  nod on that step.
- **The order is a recommendation, not a rail.** Pull any item forward whenever
  it's the one you want.
- **Every item earns its place in the climb.** If an idea lives on the home
  screen, in a counter, or in a stat readout, it does not belong on this list.
- **Checkmarks are the record.** `✅` shipped · `▶` in progress · `○` waiting.

## The compass — what we're stealing, and the lesson

We name the greats not to imitate them but to borrow the *principle* underneath:

- **Hollow Knight** — a boss has a rhythm and a tell; you learn it, then you beat
  it. The world remembers you were there.
- **Undertale** — the game reacts to what you actually did. Every enemy has a
  self, not just a health bar.
- **EarthBound** — the mundane made strange, and mercy built into the math: the
  rolling HP meter lets you act before the damage finishes landing.
- **The Legend of Zelda** — a tool changes *how you approach the problem*, not
  just the numbers on it.
- **Persona** — hit the weakness, knock it down, press the advantage. Rhythm,
  style, and the "one more" hook.
- **Chrono Trigger / classic JRPG** — phases, weaknesses, and techs; a fight that
  escalates with intent instead of just inflating.

## What this is *not* — the guardrails

Written down so I stop drifting toward them:

- No home-screen dashboards, streak counters, registers, or "record of you."
- No side modes bolted on beside the climb.
- No stat display standing in for depth.
- **No voice-pass on the question explanations.** The study content is sacred —
  it stays clinically accurate to the RBT TCO. We add texture *around* the
  learning, never *into* the answer key.
- If a change lengthens the read-through without making the climb better, it's
  slop. Cut it.

## The backlog

Each item: **name** — what the player *feels* · *inspiration* · rough scope.

### I. Bosses with a rhythm

- ○ **Examiner tells** — a visible wind-up before a hard question, so a hit is
  earned, not ambient noise. *Hollow Knight.* Small.
- ○ **Weakness knockdown** — a clean streak inside the examiner's own subject
  *staggers* it: one beat of advantage (a forgiven miss, a threat crash, a free
  breath). *Persona's knock-down → one-more.* Medium.
- ○ **The Summit as the mountain's last phase** — the final stage stops being
  bigger numbers and instead borrows each examiner's trick at once (the clock
  tightens, a miss knocks you back, it pulls your oldest misses). *JRPG final
  boss.* Medium.
- ○ **Elite variants** — a known hazard shows up rarely wearing a twist, so the
  bestiary can still surprise a veteran. *HK's harder variants.* Medium.

### II. Hazards with a self

- ○ **The rolling meter** — a heavy hit drains stamina over a breath instead of
  all at once; clear the pitch before it finishes and you survive on grit. The
  single biggest change to how every hit *feels*. *EarthBound.* Large (engine +
  balance). Flagship-feel.
- ○ **One-thing-each audit** — every hazard gets a signature no other has;
  sharpen the samey middle tiers so no two pitches feel interchangeable. Medium.
- ○ **Honest tells** — every spike, gust, and release telegraphs first, so
  dodging is skill, not luck. *HK.* Small–medium.
- ○ **A living encounter line** — one line under the enemy that shifts with its
  mood (calm → cornered → enraged). *Undertale/EarthBound flavor.* Small.

### III. Tools, not stat sticks

- ○ **Answer tools** — gear that changes *how you answer*: mark an option to rule
  out, bank a question to skip and return to, split a hard one in two.
  *Zelda tools.* Medium.
- ○ **Trade-off gear** — real power with a real cost, so the draft is a decision
  and not just "take the biggest number." *HK void charms.* Small–medium.
- ○ **A climb-shaping relic** — one rare find that changes how a whole climb is
  approached, not one pitch. *A Zelda dungeon item.* Medium.

### IV. A world that remembers

- ○ **Cast arcs** — Marta / Emil / the apprentice each carry a small thread
  across a single climb that pays off at the top, instead of one-off waymark
  beats. *Undertale memory.* Medium.
- ○ **The mountain's history, in passing** — the route quietly tells its own
  story as you climb it (the lost expedition, the porter, the names on the
  cairns). *HK environmental storytelling.* Medium.
- ○ **Systemic weather** — weather and hazards interact: a gust field inside a
  storm bites harder; thin air in the cold drains faster. *Systemic design.*
  Small–medium.

### V. The learning, made felt

- ○ **Lock-in moments** — a concept crossing into *mastered* mid-climb gets a
  real, earned beat (not a toast — a moment). *Persona social-link rank-up.*
  Small.
- ○ **Loose-stone reclamation as a combat beat** — bringing back a concept you
  once missed pays off consistently and feels like reclaiming ground. Small.
- ○ **The Sage as an in-climb ally** — a small, honest hint economy that teaches
  rather than cheeses (explain the trap, not hand you the answer). Medium.

### VI. Feel

- ○ **Sound identity** — a distinct voice per examiner and per hazard tier, so
  you know what you're facing with your eyes closed. Small–medium.
- ○ **Escalating streak feedback** — the longer the run, the more the screen and
  sound lean in with you. Small.
- ○ **Act tone shifts** — the Death Zone should feel different in your chest than
  the Approach did. *Atmosphere.* Small–medium.
